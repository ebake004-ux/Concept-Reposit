## 🎮 Game Design Concept

Project Proposal Deliverable

**Writing requirement:** Answer each level in 2–4 complete sentences unless another length is given.

### 🕹️ Level 1: Title & Hook

What is the name of your game?  
Why did you choose it? Does it connect to your story, theme, or main character?

### 🕹️ Level 2: Choose Your Genre

Choose at least one option from Core Genre, then select relevant options from the other categories.

**🎮 Core Genre:**

Platformer/Adventure/ (Metroidvania…. Maybe)

**📜 Scrolling / Perspective:**

2d Single Screen

**🧗 Movement & Abilities:**

Jumping | Double-jumping | Climbing |  Dashing | Grappling | Flying/gliding

**⚔️ Action / Conflict:**

Combat | Avoidance | Boss fights | Hazards

**🏗 Structure / Progression:**

Area-based | Branching paths | Boss towers | Chase sequences

**🎨 Aesthetic / Tone:**

(Retro / Pixel art) or Cartoonish | Serious / atmospheric | 

**🧩 Challenge Style:**

Precision platforming | Exploration | Puzzle-solving 

👉 After you’ve chosen your options, describe how your game fits this genre:

A platformer area-based game.

### 🕹️ Level 3: Backstory

Write two developed paragraphs totaling at least 10 sentences. Describe your game world in the first paragraph. Explain the world’s history and the character’s history in the second paragraph.

* What is this place?  
* What happened in the past that explains why the protagonist is on their journey?

Prompts: Is there a curse, a villain, a legend, or a mystery?

**Protagonist—the main character:**

**Lost newly hatched dragon**

**Antagonists—the villains, enemies, or obstacles:** Who or what is working against the protagonist?

**The ecosystem… I guess?**

**Other key characters:** Are there guides, bosses, mythological figures, or allies?

Who would play your game? What is the appropriate age group? Why would they enjoy it?

### 🕹️ Level 6: Player Motivations

What keeps the player playing?

### 🕹️ Level 7: Unique Selling Point (USP)

What makes your game stand out?  
**Cool art style and mechanics**

My precision platforming and art would be the main elements driving these emotions.
